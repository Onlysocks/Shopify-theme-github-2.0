# Only Socks storefront redesign

Built for a premium, practical Australian brand and an audience aged 35–60. Existing photography, products, review integration and bundle picker are retained.

## Editing in Shopify
- Home page: Only Socks — hero, shop by use, brand story and helpful answers have editable copy, links, image pickers and colour schemes.
- Theme settings > Colours: os-forest is the dark green scheme; inverse is warm cream. Typography uses existing theme fonts.
- Theme settings > Only Socks storefront switches the additional sitewide styling on/off. Original home sections remain disabled below the new sections for reference. Git can restore the original full design.
- Header menu is still your Shopify main-menu. Recommended order: Work socks, Everyday socks, Wildlife socks, Our story. Move Home, Contact, Factory seconds and Stockists to the footer (already linked there). Change the menu in Shopify Navigation; this repository cannot edit the saved menu.

## Photography brief
Existing photos are used now; no new photo or plugin is required to use the design.
1. Hero: real customer aged 40–55 in worn work boots, one boot loosened so the sock is clearly visible. Natural morning light; timber/workshop or rural setting. Photograph landscape 2400 × 1800 or larger plus portrait 1500 × 1800. Keep the sock near the centre for responsive crops. No text baked into the photo.
2. Everyday category: relaxed seated customer in the Elite cotton sock, with shoes beside them. Neutral clothing, warm daylight. Capture 4:5 and 4:3 crops.
3. Wildlife category: a neatly styled real pair on a cream surface with a simple gift box. Show the actual knitted design, not an illustration of a different product.
4. Brand story: an authentic manufacturing or packing moment with hands and real socks in frame. Only present a location as your factory if it really is. Landscape 1600 × 1200 minimum.
Use consistent warm daylight and accurate sock colours. Upload through the section image picker and set the focal point. Write descriptive alt text about the actual photographed sock and setting.

## SEO and launch checks
- Home has a descriptive H1; product popup headings no longer introduce a second H1. Canonical tags and existing product structured data are retained.
- Missing product/collection descriptions get a plain-text meta fallback; existing merchant-written search descriptions take priority.
- Review homepage title/description in Shopify Preferences and each product/collection search listing in Admin. Keep descriptions specific, not repeated keyword lists.
- Validate the unpublished Shopify preview on phone and desktop: menu, search, filters, variants, every bundle tier, cart drawer, checkout handoff and newsletter. App rendering and Shopify Liquid require the store preview for final confirmation.
- Real reviews remain Judge.me; no fabricated ratings or review counts are added.
- No discounts, inventory, product assignments or checkout rules are changed by this redesign.
